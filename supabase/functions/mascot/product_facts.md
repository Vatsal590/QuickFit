QuickFit is a fitness web app that creates personalised workouts and nutrition plans. It was created by Vatsal Agarwala in Luxembourg. You can use it in English, French, German and Luxembourgish.

To start, create a free account with your email or with Google. Then fill in your fitness profile: height, weight, goal weight, fitness goal (fat loss, muscle gain or training like an athlete), body type, how many days per week you can train and the equipment you have.

The 30 Day Plan creates a full month of workouts from your fitness profile. Open "30 Day Plan" from the home page, generate a plan, then open each day to see its exercises and mark it as completed when you are done.

Instant Workout generates a single workout right now. You choose how much time you have, the intensity (light, moderate or intense), the muscle groups and your equipment. You can also type custom equipment and the AI suggests exercises for it.

If you select an area of body pain (knee, shoulder, hip, lower back, wrist or ankle) when creating an Instant Workout, QuickFit removes exercises that stress that area and adds gentle mobility exercises. QuickFit gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. For pain or a health condition, please see a professional.

The AI Food Tracker lets you take or upload a photo of your meal. The AI estimates the foods, calories, protein, carbs and fats, and gives tips to make the meal healthier. You can edit the foods, choose the meal type and save the meal to your food log.

The Diet Plan creates meals that match your calorie and macro targets, your diet preferences (for example vegetarian or vegan), your food allergies, your favourite cuisines and how much time you have to cook. You can open a recipe for each meal.

The Weight Tracker lets you log your weight for any day and see your progress over time. History shows your past workouts.

QuickFit currently has no paid features. Its currently available features are free. There is no available QuickFit Pro subscription or paid upgrade.

QuickFit works in any modern web browser on phones, tablets and computers. There is also an Android app on the Google Play Store.

Privacy: QuickFit only collects the data it needs to run the app. You must be 13 or older to create an account. Allergy and body-pain information is optional. You can delete your account at any time in the app. The full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Training memory is optional. If you turn it on in Memory settings, QuickFit remembers your completed workouts, meals, weight entries and reported pain so your coach and workouts can adapt to you. You can delete this memory at any time with "Delete my memory".
